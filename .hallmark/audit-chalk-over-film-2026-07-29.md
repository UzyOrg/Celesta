# Hallmark audit · Celestea `/crear` · practice and transfer

Date: 2026-07-29  
Verb: `redesign`  
Route: custom, bespoke  
Macrostructure: clue swap → shared-term completion → voiced bridge → production  
Audience: Mexican prepa students, primarily on a phone  
Tone: warm, cinematic, quiet

## Design decision

This pass removes the visible AI-design tells and the cognitive jumps observed
in testing:

- Spanish replaces the English clue in the same stable text area; it does not
  open a second card, side stripe, or oversized disclosure row.
- The selected term uses a shared-layout transition from the word bank into the
  sentence. Remaining choices recenter without moving the control dock.
- Guided practice closes into a learner-paced, voiced bridge. Transfer is named
  as the same idea in a new case; there is no artificial timer.
- Production removes the inherited transfer heading and filler copy. One
  heading, one natural prompt, one textarea, and one bottom action remain.
- The existing neutral chalk interface and founder-owned wave film remain the
  visual system. No new animation library, palette, card language, or runtime
  content generation was introduced.
