Date: 2026-07-27  
Verb: `redesign`  
Route: custom, bespoke  
Structure: orientation → case → voice → action  
Audience: Mexican prepa students, primarily on a phone  
Use case: enter one English deduction case with low friction  
Tone: warm, cinematic, quiet

## Founder-approved decision

The catalog Aurora direction was rejected after founder review. This build uses a
custom Celestea system instead:

- the founder-provided wave film remains the atmospheric layer;
- interface color is neutral chalk, not cyan or lavender neon;
- the arrival no longer uses `01 CASO`, an ornamental rule, an audio card, or a
  truncated visible transcript;
- the case is the visual subject, the voice is an integrated control, and the
  action remains in the thumb zone;
- the loop keeps the previously approved true fade from zero to one and back to
  zero.
