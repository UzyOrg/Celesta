## Findings missed by the original audit

- Gate 23 failed: the solid cyan primary action occupied materially more than
  the allowed accent footprint in the mobile viewport.
- The catalog's cool Aurora accent reproduced the exact AI-tool color family
  the redesign was supposed to escape.
- The visual structure did not change enough: header, copy stack, audio card,
  and bottom CTA remained the same basic composition.
- The audio transcript was visibly truncated, so gate 49-style responsive
  reasoning was incomplete even if the button label itself did not wrap.
- `01 Caso` added another generated-design label without adding orientation the
  student actually needed.
- The video loop was altered with an opacity floor, causing a visible reset
  jump and violating the founder's already-approved background behavior.

## Brief used

- Audience: Mexican high-school students at B1+/B2, primarily on a phone.
- Use case: solve a short mystery, learn how certainty changes an English deduction, and demonstrate transfer without feeling tested.
- Tone: cinematic, curious, warm, premium, and low-stakes.

Hallmark's “20” refers to its twenty named catalog themes, not to twenty website URLs. The atmospheric cluster was the relevant comparison set; Aurora was selected because it supports the existing cool crystal identity without requiring a generic purple-to-cyan mesh. The user-provided moving film is treated as a purposeful product asset, not as generated decoration.
